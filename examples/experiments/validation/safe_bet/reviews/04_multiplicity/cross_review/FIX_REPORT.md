# Role 04 bounded fix report

2026-10-30. Candidate: `.work/ab-safe-bet/repo`. Scope: SRM implementation, its tests/docs, MDE/reading regression tests. Shared statistical primitives and scorecard source were owned by role 01/root and were not edited by this role.

## Implemented changes

1. **Planned but absent arms count as zero.** Explicit `expected_shares` keys now define design categories even if an arm has no observed assignments. Labels omitted from the design remain reported as unassigned. Typed aliases that collapse to one category are rejected to prevent double-counting a planned arm.
2. **Two-arm SRM uses exact binomial inference.** It assumes independent Bernoulli allocation at the stated probability. A 2/10 treatment count under planned probability .01 now has exact p=.004266200242831419, not the Pearson approximation's 1.55e−9. A planned 50/50 design with counts 0/100 produces exact p=2^−99≈1.57772e−30 and flags mismatch.
3. **Multiarm SRM remains explicitly approximate and guarded.** Pearson p-values are available only when every expected count is at least five. The guard is a limited asymptotic-domain rule, not a theorem of exact calibration. Sparse rows retain counts/statistic and an explicit unavailable reason.
4. **Unavailable checks cannot look like a pass.** Overall and split `mismatch` are tri-state: `True`, `False`, or `None`. Missing p-values produce `None`; conflicting assignments independently keep overall `mismatch=True`. Readings say the check is unavailable. Empty split tables retain their schema and do not crash.
5. **Strata/period families preserve unavailable planned rows.** Available p-values are Holm-adjusted over the complete split family, with unavailable rows still in its size. Exact two-arm checks work when one arm is absent inside a stratum or day.
6. **MDE and reading tests match the narrowed contract.** Root/role-01 production changes reject power below .5, expose approximate MDE, remove inverse-probability CI prose, and distinguish exact Fisher/conservative intervals from approximate methods. This role added failing-before regressions and updated goldens without editing `tools/ab_test.py` or `stats/primitives.py`.

The full initial findings remain in the original `REPORT.md`; this report does not replace them.

## Public metadata contract for MCP, docs and downstream consumers

No input signature changed. The caller must establish the stated allocation model. Supplying a roster does not prove independence; fixed-total or blocked allocation requires a design-specific diagnostic.

| Field | Two arms | Multiple arms |
|---|---|---|
| `p_value_method` | `exact_binomial_two_sided` | `pearson_chi_square_asymptotic` |
| `sampling_assumption` | `independent_bernoulli` | `independent_multinomial` |
| `inference_status` | `ok` or `no_data` | `ok`, `no_data`, or `insufficient_expected_counts` |
| `support_warning` | empty or `no_assigned_paths` | empty, `no_assigned_paths`, or `insufficient_expected_counts` |
| `expected_count_min` | minimum planned expected count | minimum planned expected count |
| `mismatch` with unavailable p | `None`, unless assignment conflict independently alarms | `None`, unless assignment conflict independently alarms |

These method/status/count fields appear in the overall result and each split row. `chi2` and `dof` remain the descriptive Pearson discrepancy and degrees of freedom for backward-compatible inspection; **they do not determine the two-arm p-value**. Consumers must require `inference_status="ok"` before interpreting an allocation p-value, rather than treating `not mismatch` as proof of a passed check. The overall mismatch excludes split alarms, as before; the overall and split families and repeated calls are not jointly adjusted.

Root was sent the exact labels and tri-state change for subsequent MCP/eventstream integration. The eventstream autogenerated docstring and MCP-facing assertions are owned by other roles; this role updated the dedicated SRM template and the SRM guide section only.
